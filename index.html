System Architecture

How VEIL operates without central servers

Every conversation follows a deterministic three-stage cryptographic lifecycle that eliminates corporate cloud storage.

STAGE 01

Sovereign Key Generation

On first launch, VEIL generates 128 bits of entropy to create a 12-word BIP-39 mnemonic locally. PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA512 derives your Ed25519 signing identity and X25519 Diffie-Hellman keypair without asking for a SIM card or phone number.

STAGE 02

Nostr Discovery & Blind Wake

Peers resolve @username manifests across decentralized Nostr relays. If a recipient's phone is in Android Doze sleep, an empty 0-byte blind wake tickle wakes their background socket in ~200ms with zero sender metadata.

STAGE 03

WebRTC P2P & SQLCipher Vault

Text, voice notes, and calls stream directly phone-to-phone over DTLS-secured WebRTC DataChannels. Incoming messages are decrypted in RAM and committed straight to your local AES-256 SQLCipher database.

Features / Sovereign 12-Word Identity
BIP-39 • ED25519 • X25519

Mathematical identity without SIM cards or phone numbers.

Phone numbers link your private conversations to your real-world government ID, telecom carrier, and physical location. VEIL replaces phone numbers with a deterministic 12-word BIP-39 recovery phrase generated offline on your phone.

1. Entropy &PBKDF2 Derivation: 128-bit SecureRandom entropy produces 12 English words. Running 2,048 rounds of PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA512 derives a 512-bit master seed.
2. Dual Cryptographic Keypairs: The master seed deterministically generates an Ed25519 signing key (for Nostr identity manifests) and an X25519 keypair (for ECDH Double Ratchet sessions).
3. Instant New-Phone Restore: Enter your 12 words on any new Android phone to recompute your exact veil_<hash> ID and fetch your signed @username profile from relays.

Features / Encrypted Local Vaults
SQLCIPHER 256-BIT • WAL MODE • DECOY PIN

Hardware-wrapped SQLCipher storage & plausible deniability.

Every message, attachment, and contact record is stored locally inside an AES-256-CBC SQLCipher database running in Write-Ahead Logging (WAL) mode. Even if someone extracts the raw flash storage of your phone, the database is computationally indistinguishable from random noise.

Features / Private Voice & Video Calls
SFRAME E2EE • DTLS-SRTP • OPUS 16KBPS

Crystal-clear P2P calls with real-time voice privacy filters.

VEIL negotiates direct UDP media streams using STUN/ICE hole-punching and wraps every individual audio and video frame inside an SFrame AES-256-GCM layer on top of standard WebRTC DTLS-SRTP.

  • 3G/4G Low-Bitrate Opus Tuning: Adaptive bitrate down to 12 kbps with In-Band Forward Error Correction (FEC) for congested mobile networks.
  • Biometric Voiceprint Protection: Optional live DSP pitch and formant shifting prevents acoustic voiceprint harvesting.
  • Zero Call Logs on Servers: Call signaling happens via ephemeral encrypted events; no central server knows who called whom.

Features / Hybrid Relay Mesh
WEBRTC P2P + 0-BYTE BLIND WAKE + NOSTR NIP-59

How VEIL delivers messages across Online, Sleeping, and Offline states.

Pure P2P apps often fail on mobile when a recipient turns off their screen or loses connectivity. VEIL solves this with a 3-tier hybrid mesh that preserves 100% metadata privacy while guaranteeing instant delivery.

STEP 1 • SIGNALING (60ms)
Encrypted SDP/ICE Exchange

Phone A and Phone B exchange ephemeral WebRTC coordinates over public Nostr relays.

STEP 2 • TRANSPORT (14ms)
Direct WebRTC DataChannel

Messages, voice notes, and 100MB files stream directly between phones. Firebase and cloud servers are completely inactive.

STEP 3 • VAULT COMMIT
Zero Relay Footprint

Payload is decrypted in RAM and saved to local SQLCipher. Zero ciphertext remains on any relay.

About VEIL & The Sovereign Manifesto

Private communication belongs to the devices in the conversation—never a cloud server.

Modern messaging platforms force users into a false trade-off: accept phone-number tracking and centralized cloud metadata harvesting in exchange for a smooth mobile experience.
